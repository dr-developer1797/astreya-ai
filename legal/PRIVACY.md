## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to visitors of astreya.ai and users of app.astreya.ai. It does not apply to third-party sites or services we link to (for example, Indian Kanoon or Google AI), which have their own policies.

## 3. Data we collect

### 3.1 Information you provide

- Account and profile details (when accounts are enabled): name, email, organisation, display name.
- Workspace content you enter or generate: matter metadata, prompts, documents, drafts, research threads, risk reports, notes, and similar materials.
- Support communications: emails or messages you send us.
- Optional billing details (if you purchase a paid plan): handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages/views, feature usage, approximate location derived from IP, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.
- Cookies and similar technologies on the marketing site and app (see Section 8).
- Error and performance telemetry (for example via Sentry) to diagnose failures. We configure telemetry to avoid sending secrets where practicable.

### 3.3 Information from third parties

- Authentication providers (when enabled), e.g. identity and email from the provider.
- Infrastructure and AI providers that process inputs you submit in order to return model outputs.

## 4. How we use data

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate, and improve the Service;
- generate AI outputs based on your prompts and uploaded text;
- authenticate users, secure the Service, and prevent abuse;
- monitor reliability, debug errors, and analyse aggregate product usage;
- communicate service notices, security alerts, and (with consent where required) product updates;
- comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. AI processing

Astreya uses third-party AI and search providers (including Google’s generative AI APIs and, where configured, Indian Kanoon) to process prompts and retrieve legal materials. Content you submit may be transmitted to those providers solely to deliver the feature you requested.

**Do not submit** secrets, passwords, or data you are not authorised to process. You remain responsible for obtaining any client consent required before uploading client-confidential material.

## 6. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location and applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other rules that may apply), we process data on bases such as: performance of a contract; legitimate use for security and service improvement; consent where required; and compliance with legal obligations.

## 7. Sharing

We may share data with:

- **Processors** who help us run the Service (hosting, database, email, analytics, error monitoring, AI inference, payments), under contractual confidentiality and security obligations;
- **Professional advisers** and authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety;
- a buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with notice where required).

We do not share workspace content publicly.

## 8. Cookies

We use essential cookies needed for the Service to function, and may use analytics or preference cookies on astreya.ai. You can control cookies through your browser. Blocking essential cookies may break the app.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data and workspace content only as long as needed for the purposes above, including backup and legal retention, then delete or anonymise it. You may request deletion of your account and associated workspace data by contacting legal@astreya.ai, subject to legal holds and residual backup copies for a limited period.

## 10. Security
